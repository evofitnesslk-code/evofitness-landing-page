About EVO Fitness

EVO Fitness (Pvt) Ltd is a professionally managed health and fitness company established by a team of professional and experienced individuals. We provide a modern fitness destination with quality equipment, professional coaching, and a welcoming environment for people of all fitness levels.

Our Vision: To become one of Sri Lanka's leading health and fitness brands by inspiring healthier lifestyles and building stronger communities.
